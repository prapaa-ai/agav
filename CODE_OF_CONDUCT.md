# Code of Conduct

## Purpose
We want this project to be welcoming, safe, and useful for everyone. Please treat other people with respect, patience, and good faith.

## Expected behavior
Be kind, constructive, and inclusive. Listen to others, give feedback respectfully, and assume people are here to help. Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and points of view.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, personal attacks, intimidation, sexual language or unwanted advances, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberately making others feel unsafe are also not okay.

## Reporting
If something happens that makes you uncomfortable or seems inappropriate, please report it to the maintainers privately. Include what happened, where it happened, and any helpful context or screenshots if you can.

## Consequences
Maintainers may edit or remove content, ask for a change in behavior, limit participation, or ban someone from the project if needed. We will try to handle concerns fairly and calmly.
